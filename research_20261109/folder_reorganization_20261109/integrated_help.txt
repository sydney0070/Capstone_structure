20261109 integrated Mars ground–RC foundation–dome analysis. InSight/ELYSE-
centred flat reference site by default, ENU, SI. One domain, one
mass/selfweight assignment and one 3-component earthquake input. Default:
unpressurized, uncovered coupling check. Not an optimal/safe design; no claim
of reconstructed InSight site geology.
